# Devink

## Overview

Devink is a developer-focused publishing platform for creating, discovering, and discussing technical content.

The platform is designed around the needs of developers and software engineers, providing a focused environment for sharing knowledge, building a professional presence, and discovering valuable technical content.

Devink is inspired by the publishing experience of platforms such as Medium, but its purpose is not to be a general-purpose blogging platform. Its product direction is centered specifically around the developer community and the way technical knowledge is created, shared, and consumed.

---

## Vision

The vision of Devink is to become a focused publishing and knowledge-sharing platform for the technology community. Rather than treating technical content as just another category of online content, Devink aims to make technology, engineering, and technical knowledge the foundation of the platform itself.

Devink seeks to create an environment where people can share their knowledge, experiences, ideas, and perspectives through high-quality technical content. At the same time, readers should be able to easily discover valuable content, explore different topics and authors, and engage in meaningful discussions around what they read.

Over time, Devink aims to become a place where technical knowledge can be created, organized, discovered, and preserved, while the contributions of its members help them build a meaningful professional identity within the broader technology community.

---

## Problem

Developers already have many ways to publish content online, but technical content is often distributed across general-purpose platforms, personal blogs, social networks, documentation sites, and community forums.

This creates several challenges:

- Technical content is mixed with large amounts of unrelated content.
- Discovering high-quality technical writing can be difficult.
- Some platforms place valuable technical content behind paywalls or subscription requirements.
- Developers often have to maintain their own publishing infrastructure.
- Technical writing and professional identity are frequently separated across different platforms.
- Developers in some regions, including Iran, may face additional barriers when accessing international platforms and services.
- Existing platforms are not always designed around the needs of software developers.

Devink aims to provide a more focused and accessible environment where technical publishing and developer identity are first-class concepts.

---

## Target Audience

Devink is designed for people who work in, study, or are seriously interested in technology, software, and digital engineering. While the term "developer" is used broadly to describe the community, Devink is not limited to software developers. Professionals and enthusiasts across different areas of technology should feel equally welcome to publish, share, and discuss their knowledge, experiences, ideas, and insights on Devink.

The target audience includes:

- Software Developers and Engineers
- Backend, Frontend, and Full-Stack Developers
- Mobile and Application Developers
- AI and Machine Learning Engineers
- Data Scientists and Data Engineers
- DevOps, SRE, and Platform Engineers
- Cloud and Infrastructure Engineers
- Cybersecurity and Information Security Professionals
- Database Engineers and Administrators
- QA and Software Testing Engineers
- Technical Architects and Engineering Leads
- Students and people learning technology-related fields
- And anyone interested in learning from the technical community...

The community may evolve and become more focused as Devink grows and real user needs become clearer.

## Value Proposition

Devink provides a focused publishing environment where people can turn their technical knowledge and experiences into lasting content.

For authors, Devink provides:

- A dedicated space for technical writing
- A professional profile
- An audience interested in technology
- Tools for publishing and organizing technical content
- Opportunities for discussion and community interaction

For readers, Devink provides:

- Technology-focused content discovery
- Topic and tag-based exploration
- Author discovery
- A focused reading experience
- The ability to interact with and save useful content

## Product Principles

Devink follows a set of principles that guide product decisions and help maintain a clear direction as the platform evolves.

### Technical Community First

Devink should be built around the needs of the technology community, providing an environment where people can share knowledge, experiences, ideas, and perspectives across different areas of technology.

### Quality Over Quantity

The goal is not simply to maximize the amount of published content. Devink should encourage useful, thoughtful, and technically valuable content that provides lasting value to the community.

### Focus

Devink should maintain a clear identity as a technology-focused publishing and knowledge-sharing platform. Social and community features should support this purpose rather than turn the platform into a general-purpose social network.

### Simplicity

The product should remain easy to understand and use. Features should solve real problems and provide meaningful value rather than exist simply because similar platforms have them.

### Accessible Knowledge

Technical knowledge should be as accessible as possible. Devink should aim to make valuable technical content available without unnecessary barriers, while providing an environment where knowledge can be freely shared and discovered.

### Ownership of Knowledge

Published content should be treated as durable knowledge that can be discovered, referenced, and revisited over time, rather than content that quickly disappears in a constantly changing social feed.

### Professional Identity

A user's profile should represent more than a username. Their published work, interests, and contributions should help them build and express their professional identity within the technology community.

### Open to Different Perspectives

Devink should provide an environment where people can share different approaches, experiences, and perspectives on technical topics. The platform should encourage constructive discussion and learning rather than enforcing a single way of thinking.

## Core Product Experience

Devink is centered around a simple publishing and knowledge-sharing experience. Users can discover technical content, read and learn from it, create and publish their own content, and interact with other members of the community.

### Discover & Read

Users should be able to discover relevant content through the home feed, topics and tags, search, author profiles, and related content. The reading experience should remain focused and comfortable, keeping technical content at the center of the interface.

### Write & Publish

Users should be able to turn their ideas into published technical content with minimal friction. The writing process should support creating, saving drafts, editing, previewing, and publishing articles, while allowing authors to organize their content with tags and manage previously published work.

### Profiles & Identity

Each user should have a profile representing their presence within the Devink community. Profiles may include information such as name, username, bio, avatar, published content, and relevant social or professional links.

Published content should contribute to a user's professional identity and make it possible for others to discover their work and interests.

### Community & Engagement

Devink should provide ways for users to interact around published content and with other members of the community. This includes features such as following, reactions, comments, bookmarks, and notifications.

These interactions should support meaningful discussion, content discovery, and knowledge sharing.

### Content Management & Moderation

The platform should provide the necessary tools for managing and maintaining published content. Users should be able to manage their own content, while appropriate moderation capabilities should allow the platform to handle reports, inappropriate content, and abusive behavior.

### Authentication & Accounts

Devink should provide the essential account capabilities required to securely use the platform, including account creation, authentication, account recovery, and identity verification where required.

## MVP Scope

The first version of Devink should focus on establishing the core publishing experience rather than implementing every possible community feature.

### Included in MVP

- User authentication
- Profiles
- Article creation and editing
- Drafts
- Article publishing
- Tags
- Article discovery
- Basic search
- Comments
- Reactions
- Following
- Bookmarks
- Basic notifications
- Content reporting
- Basic moderation

### Not a Priority for MVP

The following capabilities may be considered later:

- Advanced recommendation systems
- Complex personalization
- Private messaging
- Real-time collaboration
- Organizations and publications
- Advanced analytics for authors
- Monetization
- Job marketplace
- Courses or educational programs
- Native mobile applications

These are not necessarily excluded from the future of Devink; they are simply outside the initial product scope.

---

## Non-Goals

Devink is not intended to become a general-purpose social network or compete with every existing platform in the technology ecosystem. It does not aim to replace specialized Q&A platforms, become a general blogging platform, or replicate every feature of established publishing and social platforms.

Devink also does not aim to maximize engagement through addictive social mechanisms. Features should be introduced when they provide meaningful value to the core experience.

The platform should remain focused on its core purpose: **technical publishing and knowledge sharing within the technology community.**

---

## Future Direction

As the platform matures, Devink may evolve beyond basic article publishing.

Potential directions include:

- Personalized feeds
- Advanced search
- Author analytics
- Publications or curated collections
- Collaborative writing
- Community-driven content curation
- Integration with external developer platforms

These possibilities are intentionally kept outside the initial scope.

Future features should be evaluated against the core product vision rather than added simply to increase the number of features.

---

## Product Evolution

Devink will be developed incrementally and evolve over time based on the original product vision, real user needs, observed behavior, technical feasibility, and long-term direction.

Features in the roadmap are not commitments and may change or be removed as the product evolves. New features may also be introduced when real needs justify them.

Product decisions should be guided by evidence and experience rather than assumptions.
